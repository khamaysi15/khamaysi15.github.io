M.Sc. Electrical Engineering · Tel Aviv University

Low-power arithmetic for digital filters, carried from a MATLAB model to silicon.

I design digital and mixed-signal chips. In 2026 I built two interpolation-filter ASICs in TSMC 65 nm, one with loadable coefficients and one with every multiplier replaced by shift-add arithmetic, and took both from the golden model to the final GDS myself, with a bit-exact compare at every stage. Both were submitted for fabrication in September 2026.

Research interests: energy-efficient DSP hardware, multiplierless arithmetic, digital front ends for transmitters, algorithm-to-silicon co-design.
